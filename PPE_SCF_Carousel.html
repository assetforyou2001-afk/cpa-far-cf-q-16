On December 31, Year 1, Exeter Corporation had PP&E of $1,450,000 and accumulated depreciation of $500,000. During Year 2: acquired an asset for $250,000 via finance lease; sold equipment (historical cost $200,000) for $20,000, resulting in a gain of $10,000; net book value of all equipment at December 31, Year 2 was $850,000. What was the amount of depreciation expense used in Exeter's SCF (indirect method) to reconcile net income to cash flows from operations?
